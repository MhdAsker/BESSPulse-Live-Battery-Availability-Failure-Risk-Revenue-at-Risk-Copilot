# BESSPulse expected-behavior model card

Metrics below come from `artifacts/models/experiment_summary.json`, generated by the 180-interval seed-42 experiment on 2026-09-30. They are not hand-authored benchmarks and do not imply real-BESS performance.

## Expected Power Model

- **Purpose:** Estimate healthy site actual power and create tracking residuals.
- **Version:** `expected_power_v1`.
- **Target:** `actual_power_mw`; positive discharge, negative charge.
- **Inputs:** Requested power/magnitude, SOC, ambient, observable capability/availability, recent alarms, RTE, operating mode.
- **Excluded:** Targets, future fields, fault identity/severity/labels, rack identity.
- **Training provenance:** `SIMULATED` telemetry plus `DERIVED` causal features.
- **Cohort/split:** Healthy active rows in earliest 60%; healthy middle 20% for selection; mixed latest 20% for final testing. Fault events are held together where boundaries intersect them.
- **Candidates:** Requested-power naive baseline, Ridge linear, Random Forest, LightGBM.
- **Selected:** Random Forest, by validation RMSE and fixed 2% simplicity tolerance.

Validation metrics:

| Candidate | MAE MW | RMSE MW | R² | Bias MW |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Naive requested power | 0.6863 | 2.1175 | 0.9695 | -0.6863 |
| Ridge linear | 0.9358 | 1.2943 | 0.9886 | 0.6954 |
| Random Forest | 0.1344 | 0.2299 | 0.9996 | 0.0969 |
| LightGBM | 0.9578 | 1.1678 | 0.9907 | 0.1026 |

Final mixed test metrics: MAE **1.1771 MW**, RMSE **1.8484 MW**, R² **0.9719**, bias **-0.8043 MW**, P95 absolute residual **3.9574 MW**. Active RMSE was **1.8831 MW**.

Fault diagnostic: normal median residual **0.0793 MW** versus fault median **-1.4951 MW**; 50% of fault residual magnitudes exceeded the healthy absolute-residual P95 (**1.1883 MW**). This is exploratory and does not define an alarm.

Intended use is simulated expected-power estimation and residual research. It is not a failure probability, dispatch instruction, availability guarantee, or validated commercial-BESS model.

## Expected Rack Temperature Model

- **Purpose:** Estimate healthy rack mean temperature from workload, ambient conditions, and causal thermal history.
- **Version:** `expected_temperature_v1`.
- **Target:** `temperature_mean_c`.
- **Inputs:** Rack power/magnitude/request, ambient, SOC, 5/15-minute temperature lags, shifted 30-minute temperature mean, recent absolute power, cooling proxy, throughput change, operating state.
- **Excluded:** Current target, future temperature, rack/PCS identity, fault identity/severity/labels.
- **Training provenance:** `SIMULATED` telemetry plus `DERIVED` causal features.
- **Candidates:** Previous-temperature naive baseline, Ridge linear, Random Forest, LightGBM.
- **Selected:** Ridge linear, because it had the best validation RMSE. Ordinary least squares was rejected for numerical conditioning after an unstable reproducible diagnostic run.

Validation metrics:

| Candidate | MAE °C | RMSE °C | R² | Bias °C |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Naive 5-minute lag | 0.1085 | 0.1335 | 0.9805 | -0.0838 |
| Ridge linear | 0.0176 | 0.0204 | 0.9995 | 0.000003 |
| Random Forest | 0.8956 | 1.0498 | -0.2065 | -0.8810 |
| LightGBM | 0.4343 | 0.5427 | 0.6776 | 0.2272 |

Final mixed test metrics: MAE **0.0474 °C**, RMSE **0.4982 °C**, R² **0.9179**, bias **-0.0045 °C**, P95 absolute residual **0.0388 °C**. RMSE exceeds P95 because a small injected thermal-fault subset produces large errors.

Thermal-fault diagnostic: normal median residual **-0.0110 °C**, thermal-fault median **1.0702 °C**, and 100% of thermal-fault residual magnitudes exceeded healthy P95 (**0.0388 °C**). Unseen-rack diagnostic: MAE **0.0193 °C**, RMSE **0.0243 °C**, R² **0.9999**. These results are specific to this reduced-order simulator.

Intended use is cross-rack expected-temperature/residual research. It is not a thermal safety limit, anomaly declaration, electrochemical validation, or real-site operating recommendation.

## Requested-power delivery-risk models

Versions are `delivery_risk_6h_v1`, `delivery_risk_12h_v1`, and `delivery_risk_24h_v1`. Each predicts whether an eligible interval with `abs(actual)/abs(requested) < 0.95` occurs in `(T,T+h]`; requests at or below 0.1 MW cannot be failure events. Incomplete end horizons are censored.

Inputs are curated causal operational, expected-behavior residual, and aggregated rack-peer features. Future outcomes, fault identity/family/severity, ground truth, future availability/alarms/residuals, time-to-failure, and rack IDs are excluded. Telemetry is `SIMULATED`, features and targets are `DERIVED`, probabilities are `MODEL_PREDICTION`, and SHAP is `DERIVED MODEL EXPLANATION`.

The 14-day seed-42 run used purged 55/15/15/15 train/validation/calibration/test partitions and compared two baselines plus Logistic Regression, Random Forest, LightGBM, and XGBoost. Random Forest was selected for all horizons from validation only. Isotonic calibration had the lowest calibration-block Brier score for all horizons.

| Horizon | Test prevalence | Precision | Recall | F1 | ROC-AUC | PR-AUC | Brier | ECE |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 6h | 0.0690 | 0.9722 | 0.8537 | 0.9091 | 0.9257 | 0.8368 | 0.0254 | 0.1100 |
| 12h | 0.2141 | 0.9677 | 0.2290 | 0.3704 | 0.6325 | 0.4156 | 0.1558 | 0.1321 |
| 24h | 0.4004 | 0.4004 | 1.0000 | 0.5718 | 0.5710 | 0.4848 | 0.4769 | 0.4933 |

The 24-hour model put every test row above 0.5 after isotonic calibration. Calibration-block Brier was 0.0876 but test Brier was 0.4769, demonstrating severe temporal calibration shift. It is not fit for operational use. The 12-hour model also had low recall. The 6-hour result is promising only within this simulator.

Logistic ablations found residuals reduced 6-hour PR-AUC from 0.8707 to 0.7626, were neutral at 12 hours (0.5367 to 0.5352), and improved 24 hours from 0.5786 to 0.6782. Peer aggregates reduced PR-AUC at every horizon. No real ENTSO-E rows were loaded, so market value was not measured. Violations occurred on 1.32% of rows for 6h > 12h and 0.79% for 12h > 24h; probabilities were measured but not reordered.

Native importance and deterministic global/local SHAP files accompany each artifact. Intended use is offline simulated delivery-risk research. Non-intended uses include real-site control, safety decisions, availability guarantees, commercial valuation, fault diagnosis, anomaly detection, or alerting.

## Anomaly detectors

Versions are `engineering_v1`, `robust_peer_v1`, `isolation_forest_v1`, `residual_v1`, and `vote_ensemble_v1`. Inputs are component-specific causal telemetry, canonical leave-one-out peer features, and leakage-safe expected-behavior residuals. Offline simulated truth may select healthy train rows but never enters X. Scores are anomaly scores, not probabilities.

The seed-42 experiment spans 2,880 five-minute intervals, 32 racks, and 15 injected faults: five low, four medium, and six high severity. The held-out final quarter contains one high-severity event from each of six represented families. Thresholds and persistence were fixed on validation.

| Detector | Precision | Recall | F1 | PR-AUC | Detected / 6 | False alerts/day | Mean / median delay min |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Engineering | 0.9737 | 0.0234 | 0.0456 | 0.8013 | 2 | 0.0000 | 0 / 0 |
| Robust peer | 0.0000 | 0.0000 | 0.0000 | 0.0688 | 0 | 0.0000 | N/A |
| Isolation Forest | 0.2833 | 0.4167 | 0.3373 | 0.2824 | 5 | 17.1769 | 0 / 0 |
| Residual | 1.0000 | 0.7348 | 0.8472 | 0.7610 | 2 | 0.0000 | 0 / 0 |
| Two-of-four vote | 0.9977 | 0.2702 | 0.4252 | 0.5688 | 2 | 0.0000 | 0 / 0 |

Isolation Forest missed voltage imbalance and exceeded the false-alert budget. Engineering detected thermal drift and voltage imbalance; residual/vote detected thermal drift and power-tracking error. Each family has one test event, so family percentages are descriptive only. No detector produced pre-fault warning.

Held-out rack `PCS-01-RACK-03` had its one event detected at onset with 0.8433 false alerts/day. On six unseen-high-severity events Isolation Forest detected five but retained 17.1769 false alerts/day. Removing residual features preserved 5/6 detection but worsened false alerts to 19.4672/day and median delay to 5 minutes. Removing peer features preserved 5/6 and reduced false alerts to 13.7416/day, so peer features did not add value in this run.

These trusted-local artifacts use simulated telemetry and injected truth only. They do not establish safety limits, diagnosis, alert priority, real-site generalization, or commercial performance. Prompt 5's 24-hour delivery-risk output remains severely miscalibrated out of time and is descriptive context only.

## Deterministic availability context

Availability is not a model and has no predictive-performance claim. The same 10-day simulated schedule produced mean technical availability 0.99922, mean discharge power availability 0.99484, and mean discharge energy availability 0.45448. Deterministic requested-power availability was one for all modest active requests, while delivery ratio fell below 0.95 on 108 intervals. This demonstrates that physical capability and tracking outcomes differ.

Prompt 6 vote-anomaly events did not precede or overlap deterministic availability loss under the configured two-hour comparison; all 63 events were categorized as anomaly without availability loss. This is not evidence that anomalies are generally unrelated to availability: the experiment is simulated and detector-dependent. Prompt 5 risk joins remain descriptive and the 24-hour probabilities remain poorly calibrated.
## Price forecast (`price_forecast_v1`)

This model estimates DE-LU day-ahead price for BESS commercial-context analysis. The empirical
Prompt 8 artifact is trained on REAL ENTSO-E prices; its outputs, including P10/P50/P90 scenarios,
are `MODEL_PREDICTION`. Inputs are causal price history and calendar fields only. It is not a
market-trading claim or financial guarantee. Held-out and regime metrics are copied from actual
execution into the artifact/report metadata, not hand-entered here. Known risks include missing
intervals, latest-revision rather than publication-vintage history, one-year coverage,
distribution shift, weak tail performance, and uncalibrated quantiles.
## Model outputs in alerts

Delivery-risk probability and operational model confidence are separate. The 24h delivery-risk
output has poor out-of-time calibration and receives reliability weight 0.25. Anomaly score is not
a probability. Alert priority is deterministic and does not change or recalibrate either model.
